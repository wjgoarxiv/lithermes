## Brief and scope of the relaunch

- **Starting point**
  (1) 74 items in four glazes, 40% under 20 units a year
  (2) Chipping returns at 3.4% of units
  (3) Three kilns at 82% of capacity in peak months
- **What changed**
  (1) 31 items in two clay bodies
  (2) Rims 1.5 mm thicker, harder glaze
  (3) Nested sets, six pieces per box
- **Budget**
  (1) $186k for moulds, glaze trials and packaging
  (2) Twelve weeks from first trial to launch stock
- **Who it is for**
  (1) Restaurants and independent shops, 64 stockists in Q3
  (2) Gift buyers through the studio shop, 28% of units
- Source: Example studio product records (example)
