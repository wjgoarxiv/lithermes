# Brand and imagery

Use only supplied brand material, established product values in the repository, and explicit user direction as brand authority. A competitor page, found screenshot, or remembered identity can inform a clearly classified reference; it cannot establish the brand.

## Define the brand or mark the gap

If no brand is supplied, create a small, reversible identity from the operator and task. Record the decision and its assumption in the Design Contract. Do not invent a company identity or borrow a third-party mark.

An adjective is not a design value. Translate a requested quality into a decision someone can check, such as a contrast target, accent role, type scale, or transition budget. Use the product's accessibility and motion references for the values; do not infer authority from a mood word.

## Classify references

Record each reference's dimensions, hash, and purpose in inventory.references:

- **Fidelity:** governs the appearance of named regions only.
- **Direction:** informs mood, palette, or density; it is not a measurement or a capture.
- **Parity:** records the product before a change.
- **Anti-reference:** names attributes to avoid.

A generated image is a direction study, never evidence of a rendered interface. State its subject, composition, ratio, palette, lighting, forbidden elements, and intended slot. Keep any text in the image out of place of live text.

## Track assets and preserve identity

For every shipped asset, record origin and source path, license or attribution (or state that none applies), format, dimensions, bytes, rendered slot, and alt-text or decorative status. Check its crop, contrast, responsive fit, and failure fallback in the declared viewport and theme matrix.

Never redraw a third-party logo or wordmark. Request the source or use a neutral placeholder. Do not reproduce a real company's page as an operating product or generate an identifiable person, credential, receipt, or official document.

When a plugin asset changes, refresh the owning payload hash and prove an isolated install before claiming it is ready.

## Reject

- A brand inferred from a reference or memory.
- A generated direction image reported as a browser capture.
- An asset with unknown origin or rights.
- A logo approximation, identity impersonation, or asset with no defined fallback.
- A plugin payload reported complete while its hash manifest is stale.
