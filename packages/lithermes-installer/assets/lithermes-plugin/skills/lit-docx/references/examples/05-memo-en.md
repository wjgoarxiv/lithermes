# Summary of the change

From Monday 1 December, expense claims are made in the new finance system instead of the spreadsheet form and email approval. The old form closes on Friday 21 November. Reimbursement moves from a monthly run to a weekly one, so most claims will be paid within eight working days of approval.

# Changes by group

## Claimants

Attach a photo or scan of each receipt to its claim line, and keep the paper receipt for three months. Mileage takes two postcodes.

## Budget holders

Claims arrive in your approval list instead of your inbox. Approve or return each one within five working days; after that it moves to your own manager. Set a delegate before any absence longer than a week.

## Corporate card holders

Card transactions appear each night. Add the receipt and a cost code. You no longer send a separate monthly statement, and transactions left without a receipt for more than thirty days are listed to your budget holder.
