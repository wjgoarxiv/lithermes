# Summary

The first year met all three of its targets. Installations ran ahead of plan from January, daily readings arrived from more than 98 % of the new meters, and estimated bills fell from 11 % to 1.2 % in the converted areas. The weak point is meters in basements, where the radio signal is poor.

::: keyfigures
- **17,600** meters installed (sample)
  - October 2025 to September 2026, work order system; plan 16,000
- **98.4%** daily read success (sample)
  - Share of expected daily readings received, all installed meters
- **9 days** median customer leak duration after alert (sample)
  - Confirmed leaks only; previously about 41 days
:::

::: callout kind=key title="Request to the programme board"
Approve €180,000 (sample) for 400 signal repeaters and the change to the installer contract that adds Saturday appointments from January 2027.
:::

# Programme scope

The programme replaces all 48,000 household meters in the northern supply zone over three years. The old meters were read by hand once a quarter, and about one read in nine was an estimate. The new meters send a daily reading over a low-power radio network. Year one started where estimates were worst.

Read success is the number of daily readings received divided by the number expected. A leak alert is raised when a meter records continuous flow for 48 hours, which in a household almost always means a leak or a running toilet.

# Installation progress

The first quarter fell short because the installer contract started late. From January the crews worked at full strength, and every later quarter exceeded its plan.

One booked visit in seven ended without an installation, mostly because nobody was home. Most of those households asked for a Saturday slot when called back.

# Reading reliability

Read success depends on where the meter sits. Basement meters lose readings when the signal cannot reach a collector through walls and floors.

Table 2. Daily read success by meter location (sample data)

| Meter location | Meters installed | Read success (%) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Kerbside chamber | 11,900 | 99.3 |
| Front garden box | 3,700 | 98.6 |
| Inside property | 1,000 | 96.0 |
| Basement | 1,000 | 89.5 |
| All locations | 17,600 | 98.4 |

Source: meter data platform, September 2026 (sample)

A field test fitted a signal repeater outside twelve buildings with basement meters, and their read success rose from 88 % to 99 %. A repeater costs about €450 installed.
