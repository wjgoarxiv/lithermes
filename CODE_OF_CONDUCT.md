# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces. Discuss the work with concrete evidence, welcome questions, and respect differences in experience and background.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized content directed at participants, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not post credentials, private conversations or identifying details to win an argument or reproduce a problem.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should consider the behavior, its impact and repeated violations. A technical disagreement by itself is not misconduct.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel already available to you. If none is available, ask for a private reporting route without naming affected people or publishing sensitive details. The repository does not designate a separate conduct mailbox. Maintainers should limit access to report details to those needed to address it, explain the action where appropriate, and consider a private request for reconsideration.
